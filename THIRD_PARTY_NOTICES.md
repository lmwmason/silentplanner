## Dataset and derived material

Huilong Wang, Peng Chen, Zhimin Chen and Yuye Liu (2026), *A 16-Channel Acoustic Dataset for Passive Ground-Based Monitoring of Small Drones*, version 1.1.0, Zenodo. [Dataset record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23613504) · [CC BY 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The publisher's archive checksum list is credited to the dataset authors. The subset manifest selects original member metadata and adds verified hashes and byte counts. The release includes a derived table produced by selecting channel 0 of the 200 hovering recordings, joining nominal metadata, and computing recording-level digital RMS, clipping and geometry. Evaluation CSVs, graphs and four locally trained model files were generated by this project from that subset. No publisher experiment code, original WAV recordings, replay audio or publisher model weights are included. The project grants no additional rights in the original dataset. Preserve attribution when sharing derived data.

The exploratory extended comparison also uses the same attributed subset. Its tables, comparison figure and refitted model were generated locally.

The CUDA comparison uses the same attributed subset and generates independent project-trained weights from random initialization. PyTorch is an optional dependency installed separately. Its source and any components distributed by its CUDA wheels retain their upstream licenses and notices. [PyTorch license](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/v2.7.1/LICENSE)
